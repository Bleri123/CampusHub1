Registration for Summer Semester

Published

Category
Academic

Summer semester course registration opens on 21 April. Review the available courses and discuss your study plan with your academic adviser.

View Details

Summer registration details

Registration runs from 21 April to 9 May 2025. Check course prerequisites and timetable conflicts before submitting your choices to the Registrar's Office.

Students who need assistance can visit the Registrar's Office in the Main Building, Room 105.

New Library Access Hours

Published
11 April 2025
Category
Library

The university library will extend its opening hours during the examination preparation period.

View Details

Library access details

Extended hours apply from 21 April to 30 May 2025. Bring your student identification card when visiting.

  • Monday to Friday: 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.
  • Saturday: 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m.
  • Sunday: Closed.

Quiet study rooms and computer workstations are available during these hours.

Student Union Elections

Published

Category

Help shape campus life by nominating candidates and participating in this year's Student Union elections.

Student Union election details
